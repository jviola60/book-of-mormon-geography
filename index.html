Old World Origins & Transoceanic Crossings

Geographical Clarification: The Book of Mormon map displayed here represents the Promised Land continent. All three scriptural migrations originated in the Old World (Babel and Jerusalem) and were miraculously guided across vast oceans. The scriptural record focuses on their covenant landfall rather than longitudinal coordinates.
🌊

1. The Jaredites

c. 2200 BC • Ether 1–6

Departed the Great Tower (Babel). Traveled to the Valley of Nimrod, across ancient inland seas, and spent 344 days in eight submersible barges across the great deep to the Land Northward.

⛵

2. Lehi & Sariah's Family

c. 600 BC • 1 Nephi 1–18

Departed Jerusalem into the Arabian wilderness, pitched tents in the Valley of Lemuel, reached Bountiful on the Arabian Sea, built a ship directed by the Liahona, and landed on the West Sea coast.

⚓

3. Mulekites

c. 586 BC • Omni 1, Helaman 6

Escaped the Babylonian conquest of Jerusalem (Mulek was son of King Zedekiah). Guided by the hand of the Lord across the great waters, arriving at the Land Northward and settling Zarahemla.

Official Church Stance & Pure Internal Atlas Design

CORE DESIGN RULE

This atlas is constructed solely from the internal geographic statements of the Book of Mormon text. No external geographic model has been applied.

"The Church does not take a position on the specific geographic locations of Book of Mormon events in the ancient Americas... The best guide to Book of Mormon geography is the text of the Book of Mormon itself."

— First Presidency of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

This interactive atlas strictly visualizes the internal textual relationships, day-journeys, river flows (River Sidon), and cataclysmic topographical changes recorded in the sacred text itself (1981/2013 LDS Edition).

No external theories—whether Mesoamerican, Heartland, Baja, New York, or South American—are applied, referenced, or endorsed. Every landmark, region, and distance is categorized with a 4-level textual confidence classification.

The Prophet Joseph Smith stated that the Book of Mormon was written for our day to bring souls unto Jesus Christ. Geographic analysis is an educational cartographic aid to scriptural study, not an official doctrinal declaration.
